# Code of conduct

Be kind and assume good faith. Criticise the work, not the person.

Harassment, insults, slurs and sexual or violent content aren't allowed anywhere in the project. Don't share other people's personal information. Keep things on topic.

Maintainers can edit or remove comments, close issues and block people who don't follow this. To report something privately, use the Security tab and click "Report a vulnerability". It's fine to use that for conduct reports too.

This is based on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
