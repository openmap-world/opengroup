# Changelog

## v0.6.0-test

The first public test of OpenGroup.

- Browse groups and players from every shard, filtered by dungeon, zone or quest.
- List a group or yourself with roles, a dungeon, zone, quest or free text, and a note.
- Leaders see everyone queued for the same thing, with the roles they still need first.
- Groups from the game's Group Finder and LFM posts in LookingForGroup and Trade show up for your own shard, so Browse is never empty.
- "Post in LookingForGroup": one line in chat when you list, for players without the addon.
- Quests: shift-click a quest in your log to see who's on it and list yourself for it.
- Warlock summons, with a destination and price.
- Battleground queues: offer to queue people at a battlemaster, or ask someone to queue you.
- "Tell my party" posts one line in party chat when your group lists.
- Queued banner, a glowing minimap eye, and an OpenGroup tab on the game's Group Finder.
- The network finds other OpenGroup players by itself through friends, guild, group, whispers, `/who` and LookingForGroup, and they share who else they know.
